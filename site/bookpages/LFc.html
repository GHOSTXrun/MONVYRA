    Let's Find Pokemon! Chrystal (2009)

    Other Covers:

    Japanese cover : ポケモンをさがせ! / 5 クリスタル

    Read volume in highest quality on the Internet Archive!

    ☆★☆

    Basic Info

    Let's Find Pokemon! Crystal was published by VIZ Media in North America on May 19, 2009 and by Shogakukan Inc. in Japan on February 25, 2002. It has 23 pages and 8 full scenes. These scenes contain Pokemon from Johto and Kanto and key locations from Johto. Unlike the Let's Find Pokemon! books before it, this book does not contain every Pokemon in each scene Most scenes ask you to find 3 specific pokemon hidden in the chaos, sometimes asking you to complete another puzzle task having to do with the layout of the scene. There are a series of plots that range through the entire book which you can learn more about in extras.

    These will be the pages that contain searching scenes. Solutions and extras will be available (hidden by a spoiler) below each page.

    How I Like to enjoy these scenes is by looking for each of the 251* Pokemon one-by-one :) I find it incredibly meditative! I'll make a note of which Pokemon are missing from each scene so you can do the same!! The Pokemon Checklist (WIP) will also be a good tool to keep track of which Pokemon you have found and have left to find in each scene.
