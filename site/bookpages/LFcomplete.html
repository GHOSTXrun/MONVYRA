    Let's Find Pokemon! Special Complete Edition (2006)

    note: This page contains pages and info from Let's Find Pokemon! (1999), Let's Find Pokemon! 2 (2000), and Let's Find Pokemon! 3 (2000). Let's Find Pokemon! Special Complete Edition (2006) is a collection of these 3 books published in North America and I found it redundant to include each one of these books. If I find any information about changes made between Find! 1, 2, and 3 and the publishing of the Special Complete Edition- I will make a note of it!

    ☆★☆

    Original Description

    A note to the older folks...

    The world of Pokemon is a unique place where a variety of characters have fun and go on adventures. It started as a game for Nintendo's Game Boy system and grew into an animation series, comic book series, line of toys, stuffed animals, games, and more

    The main character's primary goal isn't to defeat enemies but to see the world, go on adventures, raise the pokemon [they] caught, trade pokemon with friends, and research the Pokemon worlds using the Pokedex. The world of Pokemon may belong to young people, but you cna use this book to play with your children.

    Let's Find Pokemon! is designed to teach your children to differentiate shapes and solve puzzles, as well as to aid their moral development.

    ☆★☆

    Basic Info

    Let's Find Pokemon! Complete Edition has 86 pages and 32 full scenes. It is composed of 3 distict books: Let's Find Pokemon! (1999), Let's Find Pokemon! 2 (2000), and Let's Find Pokemon! 3 (2000). Most scenes contain 149-151 Pokemon- few contain Mew and Mewtwo and a few feature only 1-6 specific 'mons. Most scenes ask you to find 3 specific pokemon hidden in the chaos, sometimes asking you to complete another puzzle task having to do with the layout of the scene. There are a series of plots that range through the entire book which you can learn more about in extras.

    The Pokemon featured are from primarily from Kanto, with a Johto Pokemon featured ONCE in the entire book (its Togepi!).
