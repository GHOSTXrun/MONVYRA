    Let's Find Pokemon! Emerald (2008)

    Other Covers:

    Japanese cover : ポケモンをさがせ!


    Read volume in highest quality on the Internet Archive!

    ☆★☆

    Basic Info

    Let's Find Pokemon! Emerald was published by VIZ Media in North America on September 16, 2008 and by Shogakukan Inc. in Japan on August 25, 2004. It has 23 pages and 8 full scenes. These scenes contain Pokemon and key locations from the Hoenn Region. Most scenes ask you to find 3 specific pokemon hidden in the chaos, sometimes asking you to complete another puzzle task having to do with the layout of the scene. There are a series of plots that range through the entire book which you can learn more about in extras.

    ☆★☆

    (google translated: This is the 8th work in the "Find Pokemon!" series. It is based on the latest game "Pokemon Emerald". The popular Pokemon stories are back as are unique puzzles such as "Pokemon 1, 2, 3")
