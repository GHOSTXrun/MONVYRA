    Let's Find Pokemon! Tons of Fun at the Amusement Park (2008)

    Other Covers:

    Japanese cover : ポケモンをさがせ!

    ☆★☆

    Basic Info

    Let's Find Pokemon! Tons of Fun at the Amusement Park was published by VIZ Media in North America on December 2nd, 2008 and by Shogakukan Inc. in Japan on August 30, 2006. It has 32 pages and 10 full scenes. These scenes contain Pokemon from Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, and Sinnoh. Unlike other, Let's Find Pokemon! books, this book does not feature scenes from any particular region, rather featuring generic settings such as parks and themed scenes such as a pirate ship. Most scenes ask you to find 3 specific pokemon hidden in the chaos, sometimes asking you to complete another puzzle task having to do with the layout of the scene. There are a series of plots that range through the entire book which you can learn more about in extras.

    Original Description

    English:

    "All of your favorite Pokemon are hanging out at the amusement park?on the ferris wheel, on a pirate ship, even in an aquarium! Can you spot them in the crowd? Gotta catch ?em all, but you have to find ?em first!"

    Japanese:

    "「ポケモンをさがせ!」シリーズの9作目です。今度の舞台は遊園地。観覧車や海賊船、メリーゴーランドなどでポケモンたちは生き生きと遊んでいます。読者の子供たちも、一緒に遊びながらポケモンをさがせます。"

    (google translate: This is the 9th work in the "Find Pokemon!" series. This time the stage is an amusement park. Pokémon are playing lively on the Ferris wheel, pirate ship, merry-go-round, and more. Children can also play together and search for Pokemon.)
