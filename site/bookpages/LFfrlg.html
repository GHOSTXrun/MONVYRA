    Other Covers:

    Japanese cover : ポケモンをさがせ! / 7 ファイアレッド・リーフグリーン


    Read volume in highest quality on the Internet Archive!

    ☆★☆

    Basic Info

    Let's Find Pokemon! Fire Red Leaf Green was published by VIZ Media in North America on September 16, 2008 and by Shogakukan Inc. in Japan on August 25, 2004. It has 31 pages and 11 full scenes. These scenes contain Pokemon and key locations from Kanto. 3 pages in these books also feature Pokemon from Johto! Most scenes ask you to find 3 specific pokemon hidden in the chaos, sometimes asking you to complete another puzzle task having to do with the layout of the scene. There are a series of plots that range through the entire book which you can learn more about in extras. This is the 5th "Let's Find Pokemon!" book set in Kanto! This book strongly features NPCs featured in these games, such as Youngsters and Pokemaniacs.
