    Let's Find Pokemon! Gold & Silver (2009)

    Other Covers:

    Japanese cover : ポケモンをさがせ!


    Read volume in highest quality on the Internet Archive!

    ☆★☆

    Basic Info

    Let's Find Pokemon! Gold and Silver was published by VIZ Media in North America on March 3, 2009 and by Shogakukan Inc. in Japan on June 20, 2001. It has 31 pages, 10 full scenes, and 2 mini scenes. These scenes contain Pokemon from Johto and Kanto and key locations from Johto. Unlike the Let's Find Pokemon! books before it, this book does not contain every Pokemon in each scene Most scenes ask you to find 3 specific pokemon hidden in the chaos, sometimes asking you to complete another puzzle task having to do with the layout of the scene. There are a series of plots that range through the entire book which you can learn more about in extras.
