    ☆★☆

    Basic Info

    Find Pokemon! A New Adventure (or "ポケモンをさがせ! あたらしいぼうけん") was published by Shogakukan Inc. in Japan on December 16th, 2008. It has yet to be translated to English or published in North America. It has 31 pages and 11 full scenes, including the scene on the cover. These scenes contain Pokemon and key locations from the Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova, Alola, and Galar region. Interestingly, though Pokemon FROM Kalos are featured, no scenes from the Kalos region are featured.
    Most scenes ask you to find 5 specific pokemon hidden in the chaos, sometimes asking you to complete another puzzle task having to do with the layout of the scene. There are a series of plots that range through the entire book which you can learn more about in Extras. This is the 14th and Latest "Find Pokemon!" book and the only book in the series not illustrated by Kazunori Aihara

    Orignial Description

    1400匹を超えるポケモンのさがし遊び
    リーズ累計450万部を超える人気シリーズの14作目。新登場のガラル地方をはじめ、カントー、シンオウ、ジョウト、イッシュ、ホウエン、アローラの7つの地方へ、アニメの主人公のサトシとゴウと一緒に、個性豊かなポケモンたちをさがしに行こう! 出題のポケモンのほかにも、落ちているモンスターボールや木の実をさがしたり、登場ポケモンリストを見て本全体から好きなポケモンをさがしたりなど、たっぷり遊べます。
    【編集担当からのおすすめ情報
    どのポケモンも生き生きとした表情で、ポーズもいろいろ。「なにをしているのかな?」と1匹ずつ見始めたら、あきることがありません。ひとりでも、家族や友達と問題を出し合っても、楽しめます。

    (google translate: Search for over 1400 Pokemon
    The 14th installment in the popular series, which has sold over 4.5 million copies. Let's go to the seven regions of Kanto, Sinnoh, Johto, Unova, Hoenn, and Alola, including the newly introduced Galar region, to search for unique Pokemon with the anime's main characters Satoshi and Go! In addition to this, you can also have lots of fun, such as searching for fallen monster balls and nuts, or looking at the list of Pokemon that appear in the book and searching for your favorite Pokemon throughout the book.
    [Recommended information from the editor]
    All Pokemon have lively expressions and various poses. Once you start looking at them one by one and wonder what they're doing, you'll never get bored. You can enjoy it by yourself or by solving problems with your family and friends.
