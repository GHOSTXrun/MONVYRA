    Let's Find Pokemon! Ruby & Sapphire (2009)

    Other Covers:

    Japanese cover : ポケモンをさがせ!


    Read volume in highest quality on the Internet Archive!

    ☆★☆

    Basic Info

    Let's Find Pokemon! Ruby & Sapphire was published by VIZ Media in North America on September 1, 2009 and by Shogakukan Inc. in Japan on December 12, 2003 . It has 23 pages and 8 full scenes. These scenes contain Pokemon and key locations from the Hoenn Region. Most scenes ask you to find 3 specific pokemon hidden in the chaos, sometimes asking you to complete another puzzle task having to do with the layout of the scene. There are a series of plots that range through the entire book which you can learn more about in extras.

    ☆★☆
