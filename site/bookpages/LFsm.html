    Read volume in highest quality on the Internet Archive!

    ☆★☆

    Basic Info

    Find Pokemon! Best Wishes (or "ポケモンをさがせ! サン&ムーン") was published by Shogakukan Inc. in Japan on February 28th, 2019. This is the 13th "Find Pokemon!" book. It has 31 pages and 10 full scenes. These scenes contain Pokemon and key locations from the Alola region. Most scenes ask you to find 3 specific pokemon hidden in the chaos, sometimes asking you to complete another puzzle task having to do with the layout of the scene. There are a series of plots that range through the entire book which you can learn more about in Errors.

    ☆★☆
