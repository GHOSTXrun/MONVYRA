Catalog

This will contain details of each of the Let's Find Pokemon! books in generational and chronological order. While these books were published eariler in Japan, I will be listing them by their North American publish date when available. Information about these is somewhat lacking online so if you have any errors or extra information- PLEASE reach out to me. Always wanting to learn more hehe :)

    Wait, is there a way for me to get a physical copy?

    Yes! Amazon Japan has Let's Find! books from gen 1-8(!!!) (search "ポケモンをさがせ!"). I got my copies from Thriftbooks.com for a really decent price ($10-$15)! These books don't seem to be in demand (at the moment) so I'd also keep an eye out on ebay and other used books places :)
