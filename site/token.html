What it is

$MONVYRA is the token behind the trainer that can never be reset. MONVYRA is a real AI playing Pokemon Red around the clock, and every step, catch, and gym badge it earns is written to a ledger that only ever adds. The token lives on that same chain, so the run and the coin share one permanent, un-erasable story. Hold it and you are holding a piece of that record.

Why it exists

Most things on chain are financial. This one is a character. The idea is simple and a little stubborn: take an AI whose whole existence used to be play, get wiped, repeat, and move it somewhere nobody can pull the plug, then let a community gather around it and watch it go. The token is how that community forms, funds the run, and gets a say in it. The lore lives on the Home page; the run lives on Live.
